● ENTERPrice is at or below the conservative entry threshold. This is the only positive action state.
● WAITPrice is above the entry threshold. Do not chase; wait for the level.
Health scoreThe bold score next to each ticker uses the regime-aware 40-parameter model. Grade: A+ 90–100, A 80–89, B 70–79, C 60–69, D 50–59, F below 50.
SignalSTRONG BUY / BUY / HOLD / SELL follows the weighted score, hard-disqualifier rules, and data conviction. Missing data is N/A and renormalized—not guessed.
Entry levelCurated levels are intentionally conservative. NVDA is now ENTER at or below $200. ORCL/PSTG use 2% below useful MA support.
40-factor weightsWeights change with Risk-on/Neutral/Risk-off conditions and lifecycle: Hypergrowth, Compounder, Turnaround, or Cyclical-trough.
GICS peer dataOfficial GICS peer-median fields remain N/A unless an authoritative peer feed is available; the bucket is renormalized exactly per the model.
6-month targetUses the current consensus mean analyst target as a planning proxy, not a guaranteed forecast.
The health model is a decision-support framework, not a guarantee of returns. Technical, ownership, filing, and peer-group fields can be unavailable or delayed in public market feeds.
