How to read this page

Entry zone hit

Price sits inside the buy zone. Strongest action signal — deploy a tranche, not the whole position.

Near entry

Within 3% of the zone ceiling. Watch closely; do not chase.

Wait

More than 3% above the zone. Wait for a pullback instead of buying extended.

Below range

Price fell below the zone floor. Recheck the thesis — not an automatic bargain.

The gauge

A dot on a track: green band is the entry zone, dot is today's price. Dot inside band = act.

Tranche rule

When you choose to act, deploy in thirds rather than all at once — roughly 33% / 33% / 33%.

Zone basis

Most zones are curated. ORCL and PSTG use the nearest useful MA50/MA200 support with a ±1.5% band.

6-mo target

Current analyst mean price target used as a practical six-month planning proxy — not a guarantee.
