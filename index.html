How an entry signal is defined

🔥🔥 Entry Zone HitCurrent price is inside the defined buy zone. This is the strongest price-based action signal.
🔥 Near EntryCurrent price is no more than 3% above the zone ceiling. Watch closely rather than chase.
⏳ WaitPrice is more than 3% above the zone. Wait for a pullback instead of buying extended.
⚠️ Below RangePrice has fallen below the zone floor. Treat this as a thesis/news recheck, not an automatic bargain.
33% Tranche RuleWhen you choose to act, deploy in thirds rather than all at once: roughly 33% / 33% / 33%.
ORCL / PSTG RuleTheir zones are derived from the nearest useful 50-day or 200-day moving-average support, ±1.5% around that support.
MA50Average price over the last 50 trading days — short/medium-term trend support.
MA200Average price over the last 200 trading days — long-term trend support.
Entry ZoneThe price band where the risk/reward becomes attractive enough to consider a tranche.
6M TargetCurrent analyst mean price target used here as a practical six-month planning proxy.
UpsidePercent gain from today's price to the six-month target.
Watchlist ScoreComposite quality/valuation/growth/technical score from the stock-health model.
